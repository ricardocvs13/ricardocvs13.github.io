🤔 What is deployhero?

deployhero is a native macOS application that lives in your menu bar (the top-right corner of your screen). It acts like a tiny traffic light for your software deployments.

If you're a developer (or even if you're just curious about your apps), you probably use services like Vercel, Railway, Laravel Cloud, or Fly.io to put your websites or apps online. Whenever you update your code, these services "deploy" it — that means they make your new version live on the internet.

With deployhero, you don't need to open a web browser, log in to each service, and check if everything went smoothly. Just glance at your menu bar. Green means all good. Yellow means something is in progress. Red means something needs your attention. It's that simple.

💡 How It Works

Think of deployhero as a personal assistant that constantly watches your deployments.

  1. Connect your services: When you first open deployhero, you'll be guided to connect your Vercel, Railway, Laravel Cloud, and Fly.io accounts. This is done safely with API keys that stay on your Mac.
  2. Sit back and relax: deployhero quietly monitors your projects in the background. It doesn't get in your way.
  3. Check the traffic light: Look at your menu bar at any time. A green light means everything is running smoothly. A yellow light means a deployment is in progress. A red light means something failed and needs your attention.
  4. Click for details: Click the deployhero icon to see a dropdown with detailed information about each of your services and deployments.

That's the whole magic. It transforms complex technical information into a simple, color-coded indicator.
